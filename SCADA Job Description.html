Job Description: SCADA Engineer

Company: Advanced Control Systems Department: Industrial Automation Team Reports To: Emaar COC Project Leader. Escalating To: Industrial Automation Team Leader. Project: Emaar Misr COC Location: Cairo, Egypt (On-Site: Emaar Misr Sales center / Corporate Office:17 Hassan Sadek) Employment Type: Full-Time (Annual Renewable Contract)

1. Job Summary

The SCADA Engineer is responsible for the daily operation, continuous development, and preventative maintenance of the Emaar Misr Command and Control Center (COC). This role ensures optimal system performance, rapid troubleshooting of control issues, and the seamless integration of new automation upgrades across all Emaar Misr cities. Additionally, the engineer will provide flexible technical support for other department projects as required by the company.

2. Key Responsibilities & Duties

System Design & Engineering:

  • System Maintenance: Ensure the continuous operation, stability, and high availability of the AVEVA System Platform.
  • Daily Operations: Manage routine system operations, performance monitoring, and control center administration.
  • System Enhancement: Develop, optimize, and continuously enhance existing platform architectures to improve overall efficiency.
  • Integrations & Upgrades: Design and implement new system integrations, custom developments, and software upgrades as the Emaar Misr cities expand.
  • Cross-Functional Support: Provide flexible technical support and AVEVA platform expertise for other company projects as required.

Reporting & Incident Management:

  • Daily Reporting: Generate daily operational reports and document incident root-cause analyses for the team.
  • DIssue Escalation: Immediately report and escalate any abnormal system values, critical system failures, or software bugs to the Project Leader.

Team Coordination & Support:

  • Collaboration: Collaborate internally to ensure seamless day-to-day operations and alignment across all Emaar Misr command and control activities.

3. Key Competencies (Soft Skills)

  • Effective Communication & Escalation: Strong verbal and written communication skills to clearly present daily operational reports and immediately escalate abnormal values, system failures, or software bugs to the Project Leader.
  • Collaboration & Teamwork: Excellent interpersonal skills to coordinate operational handovers, schedules, and technical requirements smoothly with fellow team members.
  • Analytical Problem-Solving: Strong diagnostic and analytical skills to handle routine platform maintenance, troubleshoot control issues, and manage incident root-cause analyses.
  • Attention to Detail & Diligence: High commitment to accuracy when maintaining detailed logs of system performance, maintenance activities, and interventions.

4. Working Conditions & Physical Demands

  • Work Schedule: Operates on a rotating 8-hour shift schedule, with shift assignments changing every month. The shift timings are:
    • Morning Shift: 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM
    • Evening Shift: 4:00 PM to 12:00 AM
    • Night Shift: 12:00 AM to 8:00 AM
  • Primary Work Location: The core day-to-day operations will be site-based at Emaar Misr project located in Uptown Cairo.
  • Office Presence: Requires occasional attendance at company head office in Cairo for back-office coordination, team meetings, and engineering design alignment.
  • Travel/Mobility: Must be willing to commute between project sites and the central office as project phases require.

5. Performance Metrics (KPIs)

  • System Uptime & Stability: Maintaining high availability and optimal performance of the AVEVA System Platform across all operational shifts.
  • Problem Ownership & Accountability: Taking full responsibility for identifying, tracking, and seeing operational issues, abnormal values, or bugs through to final resolution.
  • Commitment & Reliability: Demonstrating consistent dedication, punctuality, and reliable execution of responsibilities across the monthly rotating shift schedule.
  • Professional Integrity & Ethics: Upholding high standards of workplace ethics, data confidentiality, and strict adherence to company policies within the Command and Control Center.
  • Project Delivery: Successful and timely implementation of new integrations and platform upgrades.
